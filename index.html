No profile goes live without proof.

Every runner submits their National ID, a selfie holding it, their KRA PIN and their county. We check it before they appear in search. Scammers have to get past all of it, and genuine runners only have to do it once.

  • Full ID and KRA numbers stay private. Clients see a masked version.
  • Reviews come only from people who booked through Jirani.
  • Payments held in escrow until you confirm the job is done.
  • Handled under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

The business behind it.

Market

Kenyans abroad send home well over US$4 billion every year. Money is only part of the story. They also want things done: houses built, parents looked after, gifts delivered. That demand has no trusted marketplace yet.

Revenue

A service fee of 8% on every completed errand. Later: featured listings for top runners, and a premium plan for regular clients who need monthly check-ins.

Growth

Every viral surprise video becomes an advert. Runners share their profile link under it, and the "how much do you charge?" comments turn into bookings.
